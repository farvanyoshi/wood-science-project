## Other content changes

- The mass figure was labelled *kg*. It plots whole-block masses in **g** (rough draft Table 2).
- The load table was captioned in kN but listed metric tonnes. It now lists kN.
- The strength-to-weight table note said “millimeters”. It is kN·m/kg, and the S/W figures now use kN·m/kg (they used MN·m/kg before).
- The original `mor.csv` appendix table was malformed (shifted columns, no M/SE rows). It is regenerated.
- The impact discussion is qualified: 5–6 h dissolution had no clear effect, but 8 h dissolution reduced impact energy sharply.
- The duplicated draft paragraph in Methods (“adjustments for faster paper”) is removed.
- Appendix tables for block thickness and block mass are added. The Discussion's reference to “Table 2” now points to the block-mass table.
- The poster said the 7 MPa block had the lowest density and the MOR outlier was at 7 MPa. Both are corrected to match the paper (3 MPa and 4 MPa).
- The natural-wood control curves in `data/raw/curves` peak at 18.4 MPa, which does not reproduce the 68 MPa control MOR quoted in the paper. The 68 MPa is kept as a cited value (`lib/values.typ`).
